Welcome to Aeronautics

Aeronautics is the science and engineering of flight within Earth's atmosphere. It involves designing, developing and understanding aircraft.

Important Engineering Formula

Lift Equation

The lift equation estimates the aerodynamic lift generated by a wing.

L = ½ × ρ × V² × S × CL

L = Lift force (newtons)
ρ = Air density (kg/m³)
V = Airspeed (m/s)
S = Wing area (m²)
CL = Lift coefficient
