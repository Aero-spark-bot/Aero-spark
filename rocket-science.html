🚀 Rocket Science

Discover the science and engineering behind rockets, propulsion systems, spacecraft and space exploration.

1. Introduction to Rocket Science

Rocket science is the study of designing, building and operating vehicles that travel through space using rocket propulsion.

2. Rocket Propulsion

Rockets generate thrust by expelling high-speed gas through a nozzle. This works according to Newton's third law of motion.

  • Solid rocket motors
  • Liquid rocket engines
  • Hybrid rocket engines
  • Electric propulsion systems

3. Important Rocket Components

  • Combustion chamber
  • Rocket nozzle
  • Propellant tanks
  • Guidance and navigation systems
  • Payload section
  • Structural body

4. Important Rocket Equations

Newton's Second Law:

F = ma

Thrust equation (simplified):

F = ṁVe + (Pe − Pa)Ae

Here, ṁ is the propellant mass flow rate, Ve is exhaust velocity, and the pressure and area terms describe the nozzle's pressure contribution.

5. Stages of a Rocket Launch

  • Pre-launch preparation
  • Ignition and liftoff
  • Powered ascent
  • Stage separation
  • Payload deployment

6. Space Exploration

Rocket technology makes it possible to launch satellites, send scientific spacecraft to other planets and carry astronauts into space.
